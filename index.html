Elegance Required

Dress Code

We kindly ask our guests to join us in elegant formal attire as we celebrate Destinee's special day. Your presence is the greatest gift, and your attire will help create the timeless atmosphere of our Black Ball celebration.

🤵

Gentlemen

Black suit or tuxedo. Clean black denim is welcome when paired with a dress shirt, blazer, or other formal western attire..

👗

Ladies

Elegant floor-length or cocktail dresses in black. Dress heels encouraged.

🤠

Western Elegance

Formal attire is requested; however, semi-formal attire is gladly accepted. Western boots are encouraged or other formal western attire.

🚫

Please Avoid

Jeans, shorts, t-shirts, sneakers, bright colors, and white attire.
